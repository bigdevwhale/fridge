# Changelog

## [1.0.0]

- First release: a fridge door for two. The freezer holds a nameplate, today's weather for each of you, asks without words, thank-you magnets and trip magnets. The main door holds the shopping pad with staples, whose turn with swaps and a monthly tally, notes and morning notes, worries hung face down, envelopes that open only with both fingers on the seal, drawings, and the hideout of wishes.
- The archive inside the fridge: everything taken off the door by month, five weeks of weather, chores by month, and thank-yous.
- Sync without a server: pairing once by QR, then encrypted (AES-GCM) conversations over the home Wi-Fi found by mDNS and UDP broadcast. Edits are live while both apps are open. There is a background hum at home (offered right after pairing; "home" = a network where the phones have met, remembered by router and subnet, no location) with notifications and a "don't let it sleep" button, and an encrypted parcel file for when you're apart. English and Russian.
